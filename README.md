# Metformin-safety-and-Nitrosamine-detection-pipeline
N-nitrosamines are one of the most difficult contaminants to detect in diabetic and other pharmaceutical drugs. More than 250 drug brands have been recalled since 2018 due to the presence of N-nitrosamines within the drugs. 
This pipeline predicts N-nitrosamine presence and other contaminants found within Metformin, a diabetes treatment, and it detects the risk of the sample due to said contaminants. The pipeline also offers a risk percentage and a safety concern if other contaminants are likely to be found within the sample.
The basic framework, concept, factors, and data sets were executed by me with the assistance of Claude and Google Colab to refine the platform and increase accuracy.
